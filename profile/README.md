# Novamind Labs

Novamind Labs is the AI company based in Auckland, New Zealand. We are building Novie, the AI-native operating system: the platform an organisation uses to build, run and govern its AI workforce. Your agents. Your models. Your cloud.

[Novie Control](https://novamindlabs.ai/platform/novie-control?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=org-readme) is live. It shows what every AI agent costs, caps budgets by organisation, team and project, and routes requests for more to a named approver.
